CodeFRAME is free and open source. Teams using it at work can talk to us.

CodeFRAME is free

Apache-2.0, all of it: the cf CLI, the proof9 plugin, block mode and evidence export. It runs on your machine, keeps the ledger in your repository and uses your own model key. There is no account and nothing to upgrade.

/plugin install proof9 --marketplace frankbria/codeframe

At a Claude Code prompt. It needs the cf CLI: uv tool install codeframe-ai.

Enterprise use: contact us

Rolling CodeFRAME out across a team, or using it in regulated software such as medical devices or avionics? Tell us what you need: help getting started, support, or evidence your process has to produce. We agree terms with each team directly.

Email hello@codeframe.sh.

Teams willing to run real work through it and tell us where it breaks can also join the design partner program.

What you are not paying for

There is no hosted service, no seat count and no usage meter. It is not enterprise software yet either: the ledger is SQLite in your repository, with no single sign-on or role-based access. If your team needs a ledger shared across repositories, tell us. That is the next thing we would build, and only if teams ask for it.
